How to Read This Family Tree

Look, Warrior Cats isn't just about cats clawing each other over territory. At its core, it's a giant soap opera about who's related to who — kits inheriting their mom's stubbornness and their dad's terrible decision-making, forbidden love affairs that rewrite clan borders, and prophecies that only make sense if you know the family tree. This guide tracks the five most important bloodlines exactly how Erin Hunter wrote them. No fan theories, no shipping headcanons, just canon.

Each section breaks down by clan, then by generation. Mates sit next to each other, kits branch downward. Click any name with a link to check out their full profile. Ever wondered how Firestar and Scourge are actually related? Why Bramblestar can't escape Tigerstar's reputation? Or where the heck the Power of Three actually came from? Yeah, this page has those answers.

ThunderClan — The Heart of the Forest

ThunderClan is basically the main character of the entire series. Pretty much every prophecy worth remembering starts here, and every legendary leader either was born in this forest or married into it. But here's the thing — the ThunderClan bloodline doesn't start in the wild. It starts in some random Twoleg garden with a house cat named Jake.

1. Jake's Line — The Source of Everything

It all begins with Jake, a friendly ginger-and-white kittypet who never once set foot in the forest. But this one random house cat had two different mates, and between them he fathered the two most important toms in the whole series. Firestar — yeah, the guy who saved everyone — and Scourge, the tiny maniac who nearly wiped the clans out. Same dad, completely opposite lives.

2. Firestar's Direct Line — ThunderClan's Peak Bloodline

Firestar basically flipped the script on what a clan cat could be. He went from a soft house cat to the leader who held the forest together. He and Sandstorm raised two daughters who could not be more different if they tried. Leafpool — quiet, gentle, the medicine cat who fell so hard for a WindClan warrior that she broke the warrior code itself. And Squirrelstar — loud, fierce, the kind of leader who'd rather start a fight than hide from one.

3. Graystripe & Millie's Branch — The RiverClan Bridge

First there was Silverstream — beautiful, tragic, RiverClan to the bone. Then Millie, a former house cat who literally followed him home from the Twolegplace because she couldn't stand to lose him. Both relationships gave the clans warriors who mattered, just in totally different ways.

ShadowClan — The Tiger's Shadow

Let's be real — ShadowClan has a reputation, and they earned it. More villains have come out of these pine forests than anywhere else. But you can't understand the series without tracking ShadowClan's bloodline, because everything loops back to Tigerstar. One cat. One massively ambitious, murderously obsessed cat, and his DNA is still sitting in both ThunderClan and ShadowClan leadership.

1. Tigerstar's ThunderClan Line — Ambition Redeemed

Tigerstar's first mate was Goldenflower, a ThunderClan queen who was way too good for him. She raised Bramblestar and Tawnypelt while their dad was off trying to take over the forest. Goldenflower gave them love and patience. Tigerstar gave them his face and a whole lot of baggage. Both kids spent their whole lives wrestling with the same question: turn into their father, or prove they're nothing like him.

2. Tigerstar's RiverClan Line — The Dark Apprentices

After Tigerstar got himself kicked out of ThunderClan, he hooked up with Sasha, a rogue living near the river. Their kits — Hawkfrost, Mothwing, and Tadpole — grew up outside the clans until Tigerstar decided he wanted them for RiverClan. Hawkfrost ended up training in the Dark Forest, which... yeah, checks out. Mothwing became a medicine cat who straight-up didn't believe in StarClan, which made for some awkward conversations. Both of them were messed up by a dad who was barely around.

3. Brokenstar's Cursed Line — A Dynasty of Pain

Before Tigerstar, ShadowClan had Brokenstar. Yellowfang had him in secret — medicine cats aren't supposed to have kits — and he turned out to be a nightmare. His leadership was so brutal, so outright evil, that his own mother had to kill him to end it. His siblings died as kits, so there's no bloodline left. Just a cautionary tale about what happens when shame and power mix.

RiverClan — Where Water Runs Deep

RiverClan cats are the swimmers, the ones who actually like getting wet while everyone else complains about rain. Their family tree is basically a prophecy factory with a side of forbidden romance. It all starts with Crookedstar and his absolutely cursed life, then runs through the secret Oakheart-Bluestar relationship that tied RiverClan and ThunderClan together whether they liked it or not.

1. Crookedstar's Line — The Cursed Legacy

Crookedstar was a great leader — maybe one of RiverClan's best — but his life was basically a horror movie thanks to Mapleshade's curse. He lost his mother, his brother, and almost everyone he cared about. The only kit who survived from his litter with Willowbreeze was Silverstream. She grew up gorgeous, headstrong, and completely in love with a ThunderClan warrior named Graystripe. That relationship didn't just break hearts — it changed how both clans saw each other.

WindClan — Moor Runners & Prophecy Bearers

WindClan cats are fast, proud, and honestly kind of prickly. Living on the open moor makes you tough — these are cats who got driven out of their own territory and then took it back through sheer stubbornness. And here's the thing people forget: WindClan blood runs straight through the Power of Three. Crowfeather's forbidden thing with Leafpool? That's where ThunderClan's biggest prophecy came from.

1. Crowfeather's Line — The Father of Prophecy

Crowfeather is a mess, and everyone knows it. He loved Leafpool so much he was ready to leave WindClan for her. When that fell apart, he paired up with Nightcloud mostly because he felt guilty. And yeah, he failed both of them pretty spectacularly. But his kits? They changed everything. Leafpool gave him the Power of Three. Nightcloud gave him Breezepelt, who got so jealous of his half-siblings that he ended up training with the Dark Forest. Classic Crowfeather — great intentions, terrible execution.

SkyClan — The Lost and Found

SkyClan got kicked out of the forest way before the main books even start. Their territory was destroyed by Twolegs, and the other four clans basically said "tough luck." But their bloodline didn't die — it just scattered into rogues, barn cats, and loners. Firestar eventually found them and rebuilt the clan from scratch. SkyClan's branch on the family tree is shorter than the others, but every cat in it carries that exile-and-return energy.

1. Leafstar's Rebuilt Line — A New Beginning

Leafstar wasn't born a SkyClan cat. She was a rogue named Leaf who looked at what Firestar was trying to build and said, yeah, I'm in. Her mate Billystorm was a daylight warrior — meaning he lived with Twolegs at night and fought for SkyClan during the day. Together they had the first kits of the rebuilt clan. That was the proof SkyClan needed: they weren't just surviving, they were coming back.

2. Cloudstar's Ancient Echo — The Exile That Started It All

Key Bloodline Facts Every Fan Should Know

The Strongest Hybrid Chain — The Power of Three

Jayfeather, Lionblaze, and Hollyleaf hit the genetic jackpot. They've got Firestar's ThunderClan fire and Crowfeather's WindClan speed in the same litter. Jayfeather got the visions and the grumpy attitude. Lionblaze got the whole "can't be beaten in a fight" thing. Hollyleaf got... well, she got conviction. Maybe too much of it. Point is, no other cats in the books have that much mixed-clan DNA working for them.

The Most Controversial Bond — Bramblestar's Dual Inheritance

Bramblestar has the weirdest family resume in the forest. His dad was Tigerstar — yeah, that Tigerstar, the one who tried to murder his way to power. His mate is Squirrelstar, Firestar's daughter, basically ThunderClan royalty. So he's got Tigerstar's brain for strategy and Firestar's sense of right and wrong constantly fighting inside him. It's no wonder fans still argue about whether he was a good leader or just a lucky one.

Three Generations of Forbidden Love

Forbidden love isn't just a side plot in these books — it's the engine that drives half the story. Three generations of cats looked across clan borders and said "screw it, I'm in love:"

  • Bluestar & Oakheart — Their secret kits were Mistystar and Stonefur, and those two cats connected ThunderClan and RiverClan permanently.
  • Graystripe & Silverstream — She died giving birth to his kits, but Stormfur and Feathertail went on to shape the clans in ways nobody expected.
  • Leafpool & Crowfeather — They broke the medicine cat code wide open. Their affair gave us the Power of Three, so... thanks for that, I guess?

Two Dark Sources, One Forest

The two biggest threats to the clans didn't come from StarClan or some outside force. They came from cats. Scourge was this tiny Twolegplace cat with a chip on his shoulder and a collar made of dog teeth. Tigerstar was a ThunderClan warrior who decided the forest belonged to him. They weren't related — totally different bloodlines — but they had the same problem. Both of them thought they were smarter than everyone else. Both of them were wrong.
