What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

How We Use Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: These are necessary for basic website functionality

We do not use cookies to collect personal information or track you across other websites.

Types of Cookies We Use

Session Cookies

Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser. They help our generator function properly during your visit.

Persistent Cookies

Remain on your device for a set period to remember your preferences on return visits.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use services from third parties who may set cookies for analytics or functionality purposes.

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.
