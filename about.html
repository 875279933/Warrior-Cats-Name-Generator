About Us

Our Mission

Welcome to Warrior Cats Name Generator! We're passionate about creating tools that help Warriors fans, writers, and roleplayers bring their characters to life. Our goal is to provide authentic, lore-friendly names that fit perfectly within Erin Hunter's universe.

What We Do

Our name generator combines carefully curated prefixes and suffixes inspired by the Warriors books to create unique character names. Each name is paired with a randomly generated visual to help you imagine your cat instantly—perfect for fanfiction, RPGs, and artwork.

Why Choose Our Generator

  • Lore-Friendly: All names follow the canon naming traditions
  • Free to Use: No subscriptions or hidden fees
  • Visual Pairing: Each name comes with a matching cat visual
  • Regular Updates: We continue to improve our algorithm

For Fans, By Fans

This tool is made by Warriors fans for Warriors fans. We love the series as much as you do, and we put that passion into creating a tool we're proud to use ourselves.
