Twigbranch Warrior Cats - Character & Moral Struggles

Twigbranch's biggest struggle has always been identity. She was raised in ThunderClan, but her blood is SkyClan. For years, she didn't know who she really was —and when she finally found out, she had to make a choice: stay with the family that raised her, or go to the family that gave her life.

The ThunderClan Kit

Twigbranch grew up in ThunderClan, surrounded by cats who loved her. She had a mother figure in Alderheart, siblings in Violetpaw and Rootkit, and a home in the ThunderClan camp. She was happy —until she found out the truth about her parents.

The SkyClan Legacy

When Twigbranch discovered she was Hawkwing and Pebbleshine's daughter, everything changed. She suddenly had a father she'd never met, a mother she'd never know, and a clan she'd never belonged to. That realization hit her hard —and it forced her to confront questions about who she really was.

Choosing Where She Belongs

Twigbranch eventually chose to go to SkyClan. It wasn't an easy choice —she loved ThunderClan, and leaving her siblings behind hurt. But she knew in her heart that SkyClan was where she belonged. That choice defined her, turning her from a confused kit into a confident warrior.
