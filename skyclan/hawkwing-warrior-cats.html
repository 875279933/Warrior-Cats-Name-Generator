Hawkwing Warrior Cats - Basic Profile

Hawkwing is a warrior whose life has been shaped by loss and resilience. He lost his mate Pebbleshine when she was hit by a monster, and then he lost his two kits when they were mistakenly taken by ThunderClan. Through it all, Hawkwing kept going —for his clan, for his memory of Pebbleshine, and for the hope that he'd one day be reunited with his daughters.

As SkyClan's second deputy, Hawkwing has big paws to fill. Sharpclaw set a high bar, but Hawkwing has proven himself worthy of the role. He's a skilled warrior, a loyal friend, and a cat who will do whatever it takes to protect his clan.

Hawkwing Warrior Cats - Character & Moral Struggles

Hawkwing's defining struggle is grief. He lost Pebbleshine when she was pregnant with their kits, and then he lost the kits themselves before he even got to meet them. That kind of pain doesn't just go away —it changes you. Hawkwing had to figure out how to keep living, how to keep fighting for his clan, when everything he loved had been taken from him.

The Loss of Pebbleshine

Pebbleshine was everything to Hawkwing. She was his mate, his best friend, and the mother of his unborn kits. When she was hit by a monster while crossing the Thunderpath, Hawkwing's world fell apart. He blamed himself —he should have been there to protect her. That guilt weighed on him for seasons.

Losing the Kits

When Pebbleshine died, she was carrying Hawkwing's kits. The kits survived, but they were taken in by ThunderClan and raised as their own. Hawkwing didn't even know they existed until months later. Finding out he had daughters —and that he'd missed their entire childhood —was both the best and worst moment of his life.

Learning to Let Go

When Twigbranch and Violetshine finally found their way back to SkyClan, Hawkwing had to learn to let go of the anger and grief he'd been carrying. He had to accept that his daughters were ThunderClan cats now, that they had lives and relationships and identities that didn't include him. That wasn't easy —but it was necessary.
