Oakstep watched as Twolegs destroyed SkyClan's territory. He saw his hunting grounds turned into fences, his camp threatened by machines, and his clanmates grow weaker. Even when things seemed hopeless, Oakstep chose to keep fighting. He knew that giving up meant losing everything —and he wasn't ready to lose SkyClan. So he kept going, kept fighting, kept standing by his clan.
