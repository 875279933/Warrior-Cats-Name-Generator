Mudpool Warrior Cats - Basic Profile

Mudpool is the cat other clans underestimated, and that's exactly how he liked it. He was a WindClan deputy who specialized in the parts of the territory that didn't look like WindClan at all - the marshy edges, the swampy dips, the muddy lowlands that bordered RiverClan. He knew those places the way most cats know the open moor. He'd been walking them since he was an apprentice, and over the years he'd learned every sinkhole, every bog, every patch of ground that would hold a cat and every patch that would swallow one.

He got his name for a reason. Most WindClan cats came back from patrol with dust on their fur. Mudpool came back with mud. That wasn't a sign of being careless. It was a sign of where he went and what he did. He was the cat WindClan sent to the wet borders, the swamp patrols, the river-edge defenses. Other cats didn't like those assignments. Mudpool didn't mind. He'd rather be the one who knew the mud than the one who slipped in it.
