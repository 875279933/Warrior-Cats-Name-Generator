Hattie Warrior Cats - Basic Profile

Hattie Warrior Cats is one of the smallest, quietest kittypet characters in the whole series. Hattie is a particular cat who makes the Twolegplace feel like a real place with real cats in it, and the series is honestly better for having created her, even if she never gets the spotlight.

What makes Hattie Warrior Cats worth mentioning is the same thing that makes Henry worth mentioning - she is part of the kittypet life that the clan cats don't usually see. She has never considered it. She is, in many ways, the purest example of what a kittypet can be in this series.
