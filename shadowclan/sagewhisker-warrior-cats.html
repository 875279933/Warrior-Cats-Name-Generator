Sagewhisker's biggest strength as a character was his ability to teach. Yellowfang, his apprentice, was headstrong, difficult, and often questioned his methods. He never lost his patience with her. The Watcher of the Clan

The moral struggle of Sagewhisker's life was the burden of seeing what was coming and not being able to stop it. As a medicine cat, he could sense the troubled era that was about to descend on ShadowClan. He could see that Raggedstar wasn't the right choice as Cedarstar's successor. He could feel the darkness gathering in Brokenstar, even as a kit. But medicine cats are not supposed to interfere in clan politics, so he had to watch it all unfold without lifting a paw.
