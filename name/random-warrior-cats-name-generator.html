How the random Warrior Cats name generator works

The generator follows Erin Hunter's naming conventions. Each name combines a prefix drawn from nature, terrain, or color with a suffix indicating rank or skill. Apprentices carry -paw, warriors receive traits such as -claw or -heart, and medicine cats favor -pool or -whisper. Leaders adopt -star upon receiving their nine lives.

The tool stores 50 names distributed evenly across five clans. When the Generate button is pressed, it selects two entries at random, verifies the clans differ, and renders the results above. All processing occurs locally in the browser; no clicks transmit to a server and no history is retained.

Duplicate detection prevents the same name from appearing consecutively. Each round produces two names from two distinct clans.
