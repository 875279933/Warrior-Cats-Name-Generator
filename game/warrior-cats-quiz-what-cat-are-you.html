How the matching works

The quiz uses a points-based system rather than random selection. Each answer choice contributes points to specific characters in the pool. After answering all seven questions, the cat with the highest score becomes your match. Ties are resolved by comparing performance on the question with the strongest response.

The eight cats you can be matched to are all canon characters from Erin Hunter's Warriors series: Firestar, Bramblestar, Squirrelflight, Yellowfang, Leafstar, Crookedstar, Hawkfrost, and Blackstar. The questions lean on traits that show up in their arcs in the books, so a Firestar result means your answers lined up with the same kind of choices he made across the original series.

You will not get the same match every time if you answer consistently. Answer honestly and the page reads you the way a clanmate would, not the way you wish you were. That is the part the quiz was built to do, and the part most fan quizzes skip.

How to take the quiz

  1. Open the page. The first question is already showing. There is nothing to click to start the warrior cats quiz what cat are you.
  2. Tap an answer that fits you. Each question has four answer choices. Pick the one that sounds most like you, not the one that sounds most impressive.
  3. Read the next question. The page slides to question two the moment you tap. There is no submit button per question.
  4. Finish all seven questions. The progress bar at the top of the quiz keeps count. Once you hit question seven, tap your last answer and the result card slides in.
  5. Read your match. The result page tells you which cat you got, which clan they belong to, and why the page picked them. There is a small bio and a list of traits that show up in the books.
  6. Retake or share. If the result felt off, hit Retake the Quiz. If you want to send it to a friend, use the share buttons at the bottom of the result card.

Most people finish the warrior cats quiz what cat are you in two to three minutes. If you are stuck between two answers, pick the one you'd choose on a tired Tuesday, not the one you'd brag about at a Gathering.
