What is in the warrior cats stickers pack

The warrior cats stickers pack is a set of twelve printable clan cat stickers, all free, all built in your browser. Each one is a 512x512 PNG with a thick white border and a transparent background, so the sticker drops cleanly into Discord, Telegram, iMessage, or onto a print sheet. No signup, no email, no download limit. Click a sticker, hit the download button, save the file, send it to a friend.

Below the pack, the page has a small builder. Pick a cat, type a short line, choose a sticker style, and the page renders a fresh sticker you can save. The builder uses the same canon characters as the pack, so a custom sticker lines up with the look of the pre-made ones. Most fans build two or three custom stickers and stop there. The pre-made pack is the main event.

The pack is fan-made, not an official Erin Hunter release. The art on each sticker comes from the character portraits on the rest of the site, and the cats are the same canon cats that show up in the books. If you have used a printable sticker sheet on a school notebook, you already know the format. The pack just hands you the file.

How the sticker pack works

The sticker pack is rendered on a canvas, with each cat drawn as a flat illustration on a clan-colored background. The page adds a thick white border, a soft drop shadow, and the cat's name, which is what gives every sticker the same look. The result is a 512x512 PNG with a transparent background around the sticker shape, except for the background style you pick in the builder.

The custom builder does the same thing on the fly. Pick a cat, the page draws the cat. Type a short line, the line shows up on the sticker. Pick a sticker style, the border switches. The preview updates on every change, and the Save Sticker button downloads the current state as a PNG. There is no upload to a server, no account, no email. The page is doing the work, the file lands on your device, and that is the whole transaction.

The twelve cats in the pack cover most of the main arcs in Erin Hunter's books. Firestar, Bluestar, and Sandstorm carry the original series. Crookedstar and Yellowfang come out of the super editions. Leafstar is the SkyClan leader, Blackstar runs ShadowClan, and Hawkfrost is the dark mirror every reader has an opinion about. The rest of the pack fills out the second generation with Hollyleaf, Ivypool, Lionheart, and Brightheart. A long-time fan will spot each one in two seconds.

How to use the warrior cats stickers

  1. Open the pack. The page lands with all twelve warrior cats stickers in a grid. Tap any card to preview the sticker bigger in a pop-up.
  2. Download a sticker. Hit the download button on a card and the PNG saves to your device. The file is 512x512 with a transparent background, ready for chat or print.
  3. Download the whole pack. The button at the bottom of the pack downloads all twelve stickers one by one. Your browser may ask for permission to save multiple files.
  4. Build a custom sticker. Scroll down to the builder. Pick a cat, type a short line, choose a sticker style, and the preview updates. Save the result when you are happy with it.
  5. Share the sticker. Use the share buttons to send the pack or a custom sticker to X, Facebook, Reddit, or Telegram. The share message is pre-filled with the page link.

Stickers on Discord and Telegram work best as PNGs at 512x512 with a transparent background, which is what the pack uses. For iMessage, the same PNG drops into a chat and shows up as a sticker. For a school notebook, print the sheet on sticker paper at 100% scale and the stickers come out about two inches across, which is the size of a real notebook sticker.
