What you're getting into

The Warrior Cats scratch games are a small fan-made game I built on top of the same scratch-card idea you've probably seen a hundred times. Each ticket has nine squares, and each square is hiding something out of the books — a leader's name, a chunk of fresh-kill, a prophecy, or a cat you haven't crossed paths with yet. Click and drag across the silver with your mouse, the symbol shows up where you brush, and you keep going until the card hands you a prize.

No downloads, no signup, no email. Open the page, scratch a card, see what you've got. The game quietly tracks what you've cleared, so you can come back tomorrow and try to finish the set without losing your place.

What shows up under the coating

Every card in a set pulls from the same pool of Warriors-themed stuff. Some of it's a leader's name, some of it's a kit, some of it's a fat trout, and some of it's a line from a prophecy. The rarer the symbol, the less often it pops up, and that's the part that makes a clean scratch feel like an actual win.

Leader names

Bramblestar, Firestar, Leafstar, Mistystar, Tallstar, Blackstar. Land one of these and the card counts as a leader find.

Apprentice names

Any cat with a -paw suffix. The books have plenty of them, and the deck does too, so don't act surprised when Bramblepaw shows up three cards in a row.

Medicine cat herbs

Juniper, chamomile, dock, cobwebs, goldenrod, marigold. Standard medicine-cat pantry, give or take what your apprentice is allowed to touch without supervision.

Fresh-kill catches

Mouse, vole, rabbit, squirrel, trout, pigeon. The fresh-kill pile's been light this season, but the cards are still paying out.

Prophecies

Lines pulled from StarClan visions. These are the rarest symbol in the deck, so don't go betting your next chapter on pulling one.

Clan tokens

Thunder, river, shadow, wind, sky. The five clan sigils round out the pool and tell you which forest the card belongs to.

How to play

  1. Deal a card. The page drops you in with three cards in front of you. Hit the New Card Set button when you want a different batch.
  2. Scratch the silver coating. Press the mouse button — or your finger, if you're on a phone — and drag across a square. The hidden symbol shows up wherever the silver comes off.
  3. Clear about 60 percent of a card. The game keeps an eye on how much silver you've removed. Cross that 60-percent line and the card locks in your prize and updates your stats.
  4. Keep going or deal again. Finish the set, or hit New Card Set whenever you want a different mix of rewards.

If you're on a trackpad or a phone, scratch in short strokes instead of long ones — the canvas tracks better that way, and you'll see the symbols pop in clean.

Who'll actually like this

You don't need to be a deep-in-the-fandom Warriors reader to enjoy a scratch card. The game works fine for anyone who likes a quick surprise, and the clan theme is just paint over a mechanic you probably already know.

  • Warriors readers who want a five-minute break. Scratch a card between chapters. If you pull Firestar, the next scene basically writes itself.
  • New fans checking the series out. Scratch a few cards and you'll pick up the flavor of the clans fast — the names and items follow the books, so it's a low-effort way in.

  • Anyone with a phone and a minute. The game works on touchscreens. Drag a finger across the card, the silver comes off, the prize pops up.
