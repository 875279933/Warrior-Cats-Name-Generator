What you're getting into

The Warrior Cats scratch games are a fan-made browser game featuring clan-themed scratch cards. Each card has nine squares hiding book-inspired content: leader names, prey, prophecies, or character names. Click and drag to reveal symbols, continuing until you uncover your prize.

No downloads, registration, or email required. Simply open the page and play. Progress persists across sessions, allowing you to return later and continue collecting.

What shows up under the coating

Each card draws from a pool of Warriors-themed symbols. These include leader names, apprentice names, prey items, herbs, prophecies, and clan tokens. Rarer symbols appear less frequently, making their discovery feel rewarding.

Leader names

Bramblestar, Firestar, Leafstar, Mistystar, Tallstar, Blackstar. Land one of these and the card counts as a leader find.

Apprentice names

Any cat with a -paw suffix. The books have plenty of them, and the deck does too, so don't act surprised when Bramblepaw shows up three cards in a row.

Medicine cat herbs

Juniper, chamomile, dock, cobwebs, goldenrod, marigold. Common herbs found in a medicine cat's stores.

Fresh-kill catches

Mouse, vole, rabbit, squirrel, trout, pigeon. Common prey found in clan territories.

Prophecies

Lines inspired by StarClan visions. These are the rarest symbols in the deck.

Clan tokens

Thunder, river, shadow, wind, sky. The five clan sigils round out the pool and tell you which forest the card belongs to.

How to play

  1. Deal a card. The page drops you in with three cards in front of you. Hit the New Card Set button when you want a different batch.
  2. Scratch the silver coating. Press the mouse button — or your finger, if you're on a phone — and drag across a square. The hidden symbol shows up wherever the silver comes off.
  3. Clear about 60 percent of a card. The game keeps an eye on how much silver you've removed. Cross that 60-percent line and the card locks in your prize and updates your stats.
  4. Keep going or deal again. Finish the set, or hit New Card Set whenever you want a different mix of rewards.

If you're on a trackpad or a phone, scratch in short strokes instead of long ones — the canvas tracks better that way, and you'll see the symbols pop in clean.
