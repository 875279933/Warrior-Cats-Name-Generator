Welcome to the Warrior Cats Create a Cat Game

The Warrior Cats Create a Cat game lets you build a clan cat from the ground up, the way you want it to look. Pick a clan, choose a rank, mix prefixes and suffixes, set the fur and eyes, and save the finished character as a card. It runs in your browser, so you can come back to a design or start fresh any time.

The preview sits on the left, so you can watch your cat take shape as you tap through the steps. The controls run down the right side, in the same order as the books lay out a name. When you're done, hit Download Card and you'll get a PNG you can keep, share, or print.

How the Warrior Cats Create a Cat Game Works

The Warrior Cats Create a Cat game sticks to one rule: a prefix that describes the cat (or the world around them) plus a suffix that signals rank or skill. The pool pulls from over 40 prefixes and 30 suffixes pulled from the books, so the names feel at home in any Clan.

Picking the Right Suffix for Your Rank

Apprentices carry -paw until their ceremony. Warriors earn something that fits who they are, like -heart, -claw, or -stripe. Medicine cats lean toward mystical endings: -pool, -moon, -whisper. Leaders add -star once they receive their nine lives. Queens and elders keep the names they earned across seasons of service.

Why the Visual Changes As You Choose

The portrait is drawn straight from your choices. Pick a ginger pelt and the canvas goes warm. Switch to Medicine Cat and herbs show up by the ear. Choose Kit and the face softens. The portrait follows the same cues the books use, so what you read on the page ends up on screen.

Saving and Sharing Your Cat

When the cat looks right, hit Download Card. You'll get a high-resolution PNG with the portrait, name, clan, rank, and traits. Drop it in a forum signature, hand it off to a roleplay group, or keep it as a reference for fanfic and art.

Clan Flavor at a Glance

Each clan has its own naming tone. Here is a quick guide if you want your cat's name to feel rooted in one territory.

  • ThunderClan: Fire, lightning, and forest creatures. Think Fireheart, Lionblaze, Bramblestar.
  • RiverClan: Water imagery and river life. Mistystar, Silverstream, Reedwhisker.
  • ShadowClan: Darkness, marsh, and stealth. Blackstar, Tawnypelt, Rowankit.
  • WindClan: Open moor and speed. Tallstar, Breezepelt, Crowfeather.
  • SkyClan: Sky and cliff courage. Skystar, Leafstar, Birdflight.
