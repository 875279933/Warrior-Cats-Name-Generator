          A name alone does not make a clan. The five attributes give it purpose, problems, and habits that hold together in print. A desert clan waits out droughts; a volcano clan patrols under falling ash. Habitat decides what the cats value.

          Clan Name Rules We Follow

          The books lay out specific rules for clan names. This generator sticks to them so every result reads like it could come from the series.

          Official Format

          A clan name has two parts: a natural prefix and the word Clan. Both parts are capitalized and written as one word—no space, no hyphen. Examples include ThunderClan, RiverClan, ShadowClan, and WindClan.

          Six Prefix Categories

          Every prefix in this generator falls into one of these six categories. Nothing else is mixed in.

          Nature

          Thunder, River, Shadow, Wind, Sky, Storm, Frost, Rain, Sun, Moon, Star, Dawn, Dusk, Cloud, Snow, Brook, Leaf, Stone.

          Terrain

          Forest, Marsh, Moor, Mountain, Beach, Desert, Coast, Glacier, Volcano, Ruin, Cave, Riverbank, Snowpeak.

          Weather

          Plants

          Animals

          Hawk, Owl, Raven, Eagle, Fox, Deer, Viper, Cricket, Wasp, Robin, Sparrow, Heron, Crane, Otter, Badger, Weasel, Frog, Bee, Wren, Jay, Finch.

          Colors

          Amber, Russet, Tawny, Slate, Ash, Ember, Golden, Silver, Pale, White, Black, Flame, Bronze, Copper, Fawn, Rust.

          Not Allowed

          No man-made objects, no modern items, no human names. City, Road, Car, Phone, Train, and Concrete do not show up as prefixes here.

          The Habitat Pool

          The habitat list goes past the books' original settings, so a clan can settle a desert, a glacier, or a city park. Prefixes stay natural even when the territory does not.

          • Classic biomes: forest, river, moor, mountain, swamp, riverbank

          • Cold places: snow, glacier, snowy mountain
          • Wet places: rainforest, wetland, riverbank
          • Unusual ones: volcano, ruins, cave, city, park

          Each habitat has its own trait profile, so a forest clan and a beach clan run like different groups even when their prefixes match.
