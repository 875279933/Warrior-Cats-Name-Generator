Dawnpelt Warrior Cats - Basic Profile

Dawnpelt is a warrior of RiverClan Warrior Cats during the Leopardstar era. Her story is one of loyalty, family complication, and quiet strength.

What makes Dawnpelt interesting is the contrast between her family and her loyalty. Her brother Hawkfrost was a TigerClan conspirator who betrayed RiverClan Warrior Cats.
