Icefur Warrior Cats - Basic Profile

Icefur is a veteran warrior of RiverClan Warrior Cats during the early Crookedstar era. She's the mother of Silverstream, the she-cat whose forbidden love with Graystripe changed the entire course of clan history. Icefur is also Crookedstar's partner, and her role in the clan is both as a warrior and as a senior figure whose experience matters.

What makes Icefur important is her connection to one of the most pivotal moments in clan history. Silverstream's relationship with Graystripe led to the birth of Feathertail and Stormfur, the destruction of the old forest, and the journey to the lake. As Silverstream's mother, Icefur is at the center of that history, even if she's not the one who made the choice.
